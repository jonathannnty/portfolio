How pronounced should the brushstroke borders be?

Showing the same card + chips + button at three intensity levels. All use an SVG displacement filter applied only to the border layer.

A — Subtle

Near-invisible wobble. Organic without disrupting clean lines. You have to look closely to notice it's not solid.

B — Medium (Recommended)

Clearly handdrawn — fine liner or felt-tip pen quality. Edges have visible texture. Good match for Ben font's personality.

C — Bold / Painted

Thick expressive brushwork. Strong sketchbook quality. More editorial personality, but competes with content at high density.
